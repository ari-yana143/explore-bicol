Albay
Beaches and Coastal Tourist Spots
1. Misibis Bay — Municipality of Bacacay: A popular beach destination with clear water, white sand, resorts, and views of Albay Gulf. Visitors can enjoy swimming, kayaking, snorkeling, and island-hopping.
2. Vanishing Island — Municipality of Bacacay: A small sandbar that appears during low tide and becomes covered by seawater during high tide. It is known for clear water and beautiful views of Mayon Volcano.
3. Joroan Beach — Municipality of Tiwi: A peaceful coastal area with views of the Pacific Ocean, suitable for swimming, picnics, and enjoying the natural scenery.
4. Balaynan Beach — Municipality of Santo Domingo: A quiet coastal destination with natural rock formations and clear seawater, ideal for a relaxing beach experience.
5. Sogod Beach — Municipality of Bacacay: A coastal attraction with views of the sea and nearby mountains, suitable for swimming, family outings, and sightseeing.
6. Cagraray Island Beaches — Municipality of Bacacay: Cagraray Island has several beaches and coastal attractions with clear water, rock formations, and scenic views of Albay Gulf.
7. Muladbucad Beach — Municipality of Manito: A peaceful beach surrounded by natural landscapes, suitable for swimming, relaxation, and coastal sightseeing.
8. Buhatan Beach — Municipality of Santo Domingo: A local coastal destination with calm waters and a relaxing atmosphere, suitable for simple beach activities and family gatherings.
Tourist Spots on Land
1. Mayon Volcano — Legazpi City and surrounding municipalities: One of the Philippines’ most famous natural landmarks, known for its nearly perfect cone shape. It is popular for sightseeing, hiking where permitted, camping in designated areas, and photography.
2. Cagsawa Ruins — Municipality of Daraga: The remains of Cagsawa Church, buried during Mayon Volcano’s 1814 eruption. Its bell tower is one of Albay’s best-known historical landmarks.
3. Ligñon Hill Nature Park — Legazpi City: A popular viewpoint with views of Mayon Volcano, Legazpi City, and Albay Gulf. Activities include hiking and ziplining when available.
4. Quitinday Green Hills — Municipality of Camalig: Known for rolling green hills and wide countryside views; its landscape is often compared with Bohol’s Chocolate Hills.
5. Sumlang Lake — Municipality of Camalig: A peaceful lake with views of Mayon Volcano. Visitors can enjoy bamboo rafting, kayaking, and photography when available.
6. Hoyop-Hoyopan Cave — Municipality of Camalig: A natural cave system with rock formations and passages. Its name comes from the local word “hoyop,” meaning blowing air.
7. Vera Falls — Municipality of Malinao: A waterfall surrounded by forests and greenery, known for its clear water and nature-trip setting.
8. Jovellar Underground River — Municipality of Jovellar: A natural river that passes through a cave. Visitors may explore the area by bamboo raft, subject to local conditions.
9. Albay Park and Wildlife — Legazpi City: A recreational area with trees, gardens, animals, and picnic spaces, suitable for families and students.
10. Mayon Skyline Nature Park — Tabaco City highlands: A highland destination known for cool weather and scenic views of Mayon Volcano and surrounding areas.
Food
1. Bicol Express: A spicy dish commonly made with pork, coconut milk, shrimp paste, and chili peppers.
2. Pinangat: A dish made from taro leaves filled with meat or seafood and cooked in coconut milk and chili. Camalig is especially known for this Bicolano specialty.
3. Laing: Dried taro leaves cooked in coconut milk, chili, and sometimes seafood or pork; usually served with rice.
4. Bicol Express Pizza: A modern food inspired by Bicol Express, combining its spicy flavors with pizza ingredients.
5. Sili Ice Cream: A distinctive dessert flavored with Bicol chili peppers, combining sweetness with a mild spicy taste.
6. Binutong: A sticky-rice delicacy cooked with coconut milk and wrapped in banana leaves.
7. Tinutungang Isda: Fish cooked with coconut milk and spices, commonly found in coastal communities.
8. Pancit Bato: A noodle dish using dried noodles cooked with meat, vegetables, and seafood; associated with Bato and other parts of Bicol.
Festivals
1. Magayon Festival — Province of Albay: Celebrates Albay’s culture, history, beauty, and natural attractions through cultural dances, parades, food exhibitions, and tourism activities.
2. Ibalong Festival — Legazpi City: Inspired by the Ibalong epic and its ancient heroes; features colorful street performances and dramatic presentations.
3. Kahulugan Festival — Municipality of Malinao: Celebrates Malinao’s history, culture, traditions, and achievements.
4. Pinangat Festival — Municipality of Camalig: Highlights Camalig’s signature dish through cooking activities, food displays, cultural presentations, and community events.
5. Coron Festival — Municipality of Tiwi: Celebrates Tiwi’s history, traditions, and local identity while promoting its attractions and products.
6. Tabak Festival — Tabaco City: Celebrates Tabaco’s history and identity; its name is associated with the city’s traditional bolo-making industry.
7. Ani Festival — Municipality of Oas: A thanksgiving celebration for agricultural harvests, highlighting farming, local products, cultural dances, and cooperation.
8. Layag Festival — Municipality of Bacacay: Celebrates Bacacay’s coastal identity and fishing traditions through cultural activities, parades, and tourism events.
9. Karakol Festival — Municipality of Libon: A religious and cultural celebration involving dancing, music, processions, and community participation.
10. Pandarag Festival — Municipality of Polangui: Showcases Polangui’s traditions, history, local products, and cultural identity.
History, Culture, and Myths
1. Cagsawa Ruins — Municipality of Daraga: The remains of a church destroyed during the 1814 eruption of Mayon Volcano, reminding visitors of the volcano’s destructive power and the history of nearby communities.
2. Daraga Church (Nuestra Señora de la Porteria Parish Church) — Municipality of Daraga: A historic Spanish-period church on a hill, with views of Mayon Volcano.
3. Tabaco City Heritage Zone — Tabaco City: Old buildings, churches, and streets reflect the city’s Spanish-period history and local development.
4. St. John the Baptist Church — Municipality of Bacacay: A historical and religious landmark reflecting Spanish-period influence in the municipality.
5. Ibalong Epic — Legazpi City and Albay: An ancient Bicolano epic about heroes including Baltog, Handyong, and Bantong, and their adventures and battles.
6. Legend of Mayon Volcano — Daraga and surrounding areas: The legend tells the tragic love story of Daragang Magayon and Panganoron; their story is associated with the formation of Mayon Volcano.
7. Abaca-Weaving Tradition — Tiwi, Malinao, and other areas: Abaca fibers are used to make bags, mats, ropes, baskets, and other handicrafts.
8. Bicolano Religious and Fiesta Traditions — Province of Albay: Religious processions, town fiestas, folk dances, traditional food, and community celebrations reflect the connection between faith, family, and local identity.
Camarines Norte
Beaches and Coastal Tourist Spots
1. Bagasbas Beach — Daet: A well-known beach with a long shoreline, strong waves, surfing, beach walks, and beautiful sunsets.
2. Calaguas Islands — near Vinzons: Famous for white sand, clear blue water, peaceful surroundings, and scenic landscapes. Mahabang Buhangin is one of the best-known beaches.
3. Tinaga Island — Calaguas group: Known for its long white-sand beach, clear water, camping areas, and sea views.
4. Maculabo Island — Paracale: Known for white-sand beaches, clear waters, coral areas, and a peaceful island environment; visitors may swim, snorkel, tour, or camp.
5. Apuao Grande Island — Mercedes: A popular island destination with white sand, clear water, green landscapes, and a quiet coastal environment.
6. Apuao Pequena Island — near Mercedes: A smaller island near Apuao Grande, known for its peaceful beach, clear water, rock formations, and natural surroundings.
7. Siete Pecados Islands — near Mercedes: A group of small islands known for clear water, coral reefs, beaches, and island-hopping.
8. Colasi Beach — Mercedes: A quiet beach with coastal scenery and a peaceful atmosphere away from crowded tourist areas.
9. Bagasbas Coastal Area — Daet: Popular for sunset viewing, beach walks, local food, outdoor activities, and seaside events.
10. Lanot Beach — Camarines Norte: A coastal destination known for quiet surroundings, natural scenery, and a relaxing beach environment.
Tourist Spots on Land
1. Parroquia de San Juan Bautista — Daet: A historic church connected with the religious and colonial history of Camarines Norte.
2. First Rizal Monument — Daet: Built in honor of Dr. José Rizal and recognized as the first monument erected for the national hero in the Philippines.
3. Calaguas Island Hills — Calaguas: Green hills and scenic viewpoints offer opportunities for hiking, photography, and views of the sea and nearby islands.
4. Malatap Falls — Basud: A natural waterfall surrounded by greenery, suitable for swimming, nature trips, and relaxation.
5. Bagasbas Surfing Area — Daet: An outdoor attraction known for its waves and visited by beginner and experienced surfers.
6. Mercedes Fish Port — Mercedes: A place to observe the fishing industry, boats, seafood trading, and daily activities of coastal communities.
7. Camarines Norte Provincial Capitol Complex — Daet: Represents the province’s government and development history and is near other public and historical areas.
8. Daet Heritage and Town Center — Daet: Old structures, churches, public spaces, local markets, and cultural areas show the history of the provincial capital.
9. Paracale Gold-Mining Areas — Paracale: Reflect the town’s long gold-mining history and the importance of mining to its economy.
10. Mampurog River — San Lorenzo Ruiz: A natural attraction known for clear water, rocks, greenery, and areas suitable for swimming and outdoor activities.
Food
1. Queen Pineapple: Camarines Norte is known for its sweet Queen pineapple, sold fresh or used in juices, desserts, jams, and other products.
2. Pineapple Products: Local producers make pineapple jam, candies, dried pineapple, pastries, juice, vinegar, and other products.
3. Fresh Seafood: Fish, crabs, shrimp, squid, shellfish, and other seafood are important products of coastal communities.
4. Sinantomas: A traditional Bicolano dish usually prepared with pork, coconut milk, spices, and local ingredients.
5. Native Rice Cakes: Traditional snacks such as puto, bibingka, and suman, made with rice, coconut, sugar, and banana leaves.
Festivals
1. Pinyasan Festival — Daet: The province’s best-known festival, celebrating the sweet Queen pineapple through street dancing, cultural performances, parades, food fairs, trade exhibits, and agricultural displays.
2. Bantayog Festival — Daet: Associated with Daet’s history, community identity, and the First Rizal Monument.
3. Araw ng Camarines Norte — Province of Camarines Norte: Celebrates the province’s founding and identity through cultural programs, public events, and community activities.
4. Kadagatan Festival — Mercedes: Celebrates the sea, fishing communities, marine resources, and coastal culture.
5. Paroy Festival — Paracale: Highlights the town’s history, gold-mining heritage, religious traditions, and local identity.
6. Isla Festival — island communities: Promotes the province’s islands, beaches, marine resources, and coastal tourism.
7. Municipal Town Fiestas — throughout the province: Patron-saint celebrations typically include religious processions, performances, sports, food fairs, concerts, and community gatherings.
8. Sea and Surfing Events — especially Bagasbas and Mercedes: Surfing competitions, beach activities, coastal cleanups, and marine-tourism events celebrate the province’s coastal identity.
History and Culture
1. The First Rizal Monument — Daet: An important historical landmark honoring Dr. José Rizal and representing the province’s connection with Philippine nationalism.
2. Spanish Colonial Churches — throughout the province: Historic churches, including Parroquia de San Juan Bautista, show Spanish colonial influence and the spread of Christianity.
3. Gold-Mining History — Paracale: Gold mining has long influenced the town’s livelihood, economy, culture, and development.
4. Fishing and Coastal Heritage — coastal municipalities: Fishing provides food, income, transportation, and cultural traditions in Mercedes, Paracale, Jose Panganiban, Capalonga, and other communities.
5. Island and Maritime Culture — island communities: Calaguas, Apuao, Maculabo, and Siete Pecados contribute to a maritime culture shaped by boats, fishing, island travel, and marine resources.
6. Queen Pineapple Agriculture — province-wide: Pineapple production supports farmers, local businesses, food producers, and tourism.
7. Camarines Norte during World War II: The province was affected by Japanese occupation, military activity, evacuation, and resistance movements; local stories and memorials preserve this history.
8. Bicolano Culture — throughout the province: Family relationships, religious fiestas, spicy and coconut-based food, music, dancing, and community cooperation are important traditions.
9. Daet as the Provincial Center — Daet: Daet is a center for government, education, commerce, transportation, and tourism, with public, religious, historical, and commercial sites.
10. Natural Disaster and Environmental History — coastal areas: The Pacific-facing province is vulnerable to typhoons, strong waves, and flooding; communities emphasize disaster preparation and environmental protection.
Camarines Sur
Beaches and Coastal Tourist Spots
1. Caramoan Islands — Municipality of Caramoan: Known for beaches, limestone cliffs, clear waters, caves, and small islands; popular for island-hopping, swimming, snorkeling, and kayaking.
2. Gota Beach — Municipality of Caramoan: Surrounded by green hills, limestone formations, and calm waters; one of Caramoan’s best-known beach destinations.
3. Atulayan Island — Municipality of Sagñay: An island with white sand, clear seawater, and peaceful surroundings, suitable for swimming, snorkeling, and relaxation.
4. Sabang Beach — Municipality of San Jose: A coastal area and common starting point for boat trips to the Caramoan Islands, with views of the sea and nearby islands.
5. Nato Beach — Municipality of Sagñay: A quiet coastal destination with views of the Pacific Ocean, suitable for picnics and simple beach activities.
Tourist Spots on Land
1. Mount Isarog — Naga City, Pili, and nearby municipalities: A forest-covered volcano with rich biodiversity, streams, waterfalls, and hiking trails; an important natural landmark of Camarines Sur.
2. Malabsay Falls — Naga City: Located at the foot of Mount Isarog, with cool water, natural pools, and a forested environment.
3. Panicuason Hot Spring Resort — Naga City: Offers hot and cold pools, natural scenery, zip lines, and outdoor activities.
4. Camsur Watersports Complex — Pili: Known for wakeboarding and other water activities, with recreational facilities for visitors and athletes.
5. Haciendas de Naga — Naga City: A family-friendly attraction with gardens, pools, recreational areas, and nature-based activities.
Food
1. Sinapot: A snack made from saba bananas coated in flour or batter and fried until golden brown.
Festivals
1. Peñafrancia Festival — Naga City: A major religious celebration honoring Our Lady of Peñafrancia, with novena masses, processions, cultural events, and a fluvial procession.
2. Voyadores Festival — Naga City: Features devotees carrying the image of Our Lady of Peñafrancia from the basilica to the cathedral and forms part of the Peñafrancia celebration.
3. Kaogma Festival — Province of Camarines Sur: Celebrates the province’s history, culture, food, music, tourism, and local products.
4. Tinagba Festival — Iriga City: A harvest and thanksgiving celebration featuring decorated carabao carts, cultural dances, parades, and local products.
5. Boa-Bowaan Festival — Nabua: Highlights Nabua’s traditions, history, food, and cultural identity.
History and Culture
1. Basilica Minore of Our Lady of Peñafrancia — Naga City: One of Bicol’s most important religious landmarks and a major pilgrimage site for devotees of Our Lady of Peñafrancia.
2. Naga Metropolitan Cathedral — Naga City: A historic church and important center of Catholic faith in the region.
3. Holy Rosary Minor Seminary — Naga City: A historic religious and educational institution that has contributed to Catholic education in Bicol.
4. Museo ni Jesse Robredo — Naga City: Honors the life, public service, and achievements of Jesse Robredo, former Naga mayor and national government official.
5. Bicolano Religious Traditions — Naga City and nearby municipalities: Processions, fiestas, devotional activities, and community participation are especially visible during the Peñafrancia Festival.
Catanduanes
Beaches and Coastal Tourist Spots

4. Talisoy Beach — Virac: A peaceful coastal destination with clear water, accessible from Virac and visited by locals and tourists.
5. Balacay Point — Baras: A coastal viewpoint with cliffs, green hills, and wide views of the Pacific Ocean.
Tourist Spots on Land

2. Nahulugan Falls — Gigmoto: A waterfall surrounded by forests and rock formations, offering cool water and natural scenery.
3. Hicming Falls — Baras: A natural waterfall surrounded by trees and rocky landscapes, suitable for nature trips.
4. Bato River — Bato: A scenic natural attraction connected with the historic town of Bato, offering a peaceful setting for sightseeing.
5. Catanduanes Scenic Hills — Virac and Baras: Hills and grasslands offer wide views of the Pacific Ocean, farmlands, and coastal communities.

Masbate
Beaches and Coastal Tourist Spots
1. Buntod Reef Marine Sanctuary — Masbate City: Known for its sandbar, coral reefs, clear water, and marine life; popular for snorkeling and environmental tourism.
2. Palani Beach — Balud: A long stretch of white sand and peaceful coastal scenery, suitable for swimming, camping, and relaxation.
3. Jintotolo Island — Balud: An island with beaches, coral reefs, and the historic Jintotolo Lighthouse, accessible by boat.
4. Tinigban Beach — Mandaon: Known for clear water and quiet surroundings, attractive to visitors seeking a less crowded beach.
5. Halea Nature Park — Monreal, Ticao Island: Has clear seawater, rock formations, coral reefs, and marine life; popular for swimming, snorkeling, and island-hopping.
Tourist Spots on Land
1. Kalanay Falls — Aroroy: A natural waterfall surrounded by forests and rock formations, suitable for swimming and nature trips.
2. Pawa Mangrove Park — Masbate City: A conservation area protecting mangrove trees and coastal ecosystems, where visitors can learn about their importance.
3. Mobo Hills — Mobo: Rolling landscapes and elevated areas provide views of the surrounding countryside and communities.
4. Ticao Island Caves and Rock Formations — Monreal: Ticao Island is known for caves, cliffs, rock formations, and scenic coastal landscapes, often visited by boat tour.
5. Masbate Rodeo Grounds — Masbate City: Used for cattle-riding events and demonstrations, representing the province’s ranching and cowboy culture.
Food
1. Masbate Beef: Cattle raising is important in Masbate, making locally produced beef a key food product used in steaks, soups, grilled dishes, and other meals.
2. Binot-ong: A traditional rice delicacy prepared and wrapped in leaves, commonly associated with celebrations and special occasions.
3. Taba ng Talangka: Salted crab fat used as a spread or flavorful ingredient in rice and seafood dishes.
4. Moron: A sticky-rice delicacy often flavored with chocolate or cocoa and wrapped in banana leaves.
Festivals
1. Rodeo Masbateño — Masbate City: The province’s best-known festival, celebrating cattle raising, horse riding, ranching, and cowboy skills.
2. Kalilang Festival — Masbate City: Celebrates Masbateño history, culture, music, food, and identity.
3. Lapay Bantigue Dance Festival — Masbate City: Features the traditional Lapay Bantigue dance, which imitates the graceful movements of seagulls.
4. Pagdayao Festival — various municipalities: A thanksgiving celebration involving cultural presentations, music, dances, and community activities.
5. Katunggan Festival — coastal municipalities: Promotes mangrove protection, environmental conservation, and the importance of coastal resources.
History and Culture
1. Kalanay Archaeological Site — Aroroy: Provides evidence of early communities and ancient cultural activities in the Philippines.
2. San Antonio de Padua Church — Masbate City: An important religious landmark reflecting the development of Catholic traditions in Masbate.
3. Jintotolo Lighthouse — Balud: A historic navigation structure that guided ships traveling through waters around Jintotolo Island.
4. Masbateño Rodeo Culture — Masbate City and ranching municipalities: Cattle raising, horse riding, ranch work, and cowboy skills are distinctive parts of Masbateño identity.
5. Pawa Mangrove Conservation — Masbate City: Shows how local communities protect coastal ecosystems while supporting fishing and tourism.
Sorsogon
Beaches and Coastal Tourist Spots
1. Subic Beach — Matnog: Famous for pinkish-white sand, clear water, and beautiful rock formations.
2. Tikling Island — Matnog: Known for white sand, turquoise water, and natural coastal scenery, usually reached by boat.
3. Calintaan Island — Matnog: Known for beaches, caves, lagoons, and clear waters; a popular island-hopping destination.
4. Tolong Gapo Beach — Gubat: A quiet coastal destination with a wide shoreline, suitable for swimming, picnics, and relaxation.
5. Panumbagan Sandbar — Prieto Diaz: Known for shallow water and beautiful sand formations; best visited during low tide.
Tourist Spots on Land
1. Bulusan Volcano Natural Park — Bulusan: Includes forests, hiking trails, waterfalls, and Bulusan Lake; an important natural and ecological attraction.
2. Paguriran Island and Lagoon — Bacon, Sorsogon City: A natural rock formation surrounded by seawater. During low tide, visitors may walk through shallow water to reach the island and lagoon.
3. Irosin Hot and Cold Springs — Irosin: Natural hot and cold springs near Bulusan Volcano, used for relaxation and recreation.
4. Ogod River — Donsol: A scenic river with mangroves and calm waterways, where visitors can enjoy boat tours and observe nature.
5. Barcelona Ruins Park — Barcelona: Contains old Spanish-period structures, stone walls, and coastal heritage sites that reflect the municipality’s colonial history.
Food
1. Kinunot: Fish or stingray cooked with coconut milk, chili, and leafy vegetables such as malunggay.
2. Baluko: A local shellfish commonly prepared as a seafood dish, sometimes cooked with vegetables, spices, or coconut-based ingredients.
Festivals
1. Kasanggayahan Festival — Sorsogon City and province: Celebrates Sorsogon’s history, foundation, culture, and identity through parades, cultural dances, exhibits, and competitions.
2. Pili Festival — Sorsogon: Highlights pili nuts and other local products through food exhibits, cooking activities, and cultural presentations.
3. Sarakiki-Hadang Festival — Sorsogon: Features costumes, dances, rituals, music, and cultural performances based on local traditions.
4. Padaraw Festival — various municipalities: A thanksgiving and cultural celebration with performances, religious activities, and local events.
5. Butanding Festival — Donsol: Celebrates the whale shark and promotes its protection, responsible tourism, and marine conservation.
History and Culture
1. Barcelona Church and Ruins — Barcelona: Old church ruins, a convent, and stone structures reflect the Spanish-period history of the municipality.
2. Juban Ancestral Houses — Juban: Show traditional architecture, lifestyle, and the social history of old Sorsogon families.
3. Prieto Diaz Mangrove Forest — Prieto Diaz: An environmental and cultural attraction that supports fishing communities and coastal ecosystems.
