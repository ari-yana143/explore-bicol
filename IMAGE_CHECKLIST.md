# Explore Bicol image checklist

Use a photo that shows the named place, dish, festival, or heritage subject. Use a free, properly licensed source (Wikimedia Commons, Unsplash, Pexels) or your own image, and record the author, source page, and license in `images/PHOTO-CREDITS.txt` and the corresponding `imageCredit` in `js/data.js`. Missing files intentionally show a local gradient fallback.
